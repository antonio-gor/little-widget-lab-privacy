This policy explains how Little Widget Lab handles information in the app and on this support site.

Your designs and images

Saved designs, selected photos, wallpaper screenshots, and cached component data are stored locally in the app’s shared storage. They may be included in device backups according to your system settings. The app does not provide its own cross-device cloud sync.

The access you choose

You choose whether to connect Calendar, Location, Apple Music, or Motion. Calendar access supports displaying events from your selected calendars; the app does not modify your events. Current Location supplies one-shot weather fixes rather than continuous tracking. You can choose a city instead. Music access supports selected music, recent history, current Apple Music playback, artwork, and playback controls. Motion is used only for active Gravity mode and is not stored. The system photo picker lets you select images without granting access to your entire photo library.

Apple services

Weather, city search, Apple Music, and artwork requests use Apple’s services. Those services receive the requests needed to provide their features, such as a selected place or weather coordinates and music searches. Apple’s terms and privacy policies govern its processing. Little Widget Lab does not send your designs, calendar events, photos, or listening history to a developer-operated server.

Local diagnostics

The app can save local technical reports to help identify a failed service request. These reports contain the requesting app identifier, time, and error-domain/code information, not authentication tokens, precise coordinates, account details, or raw request URLs. The app does not automatically upload these reports to us.

Support and beta feedback

If you email us, we receive the information you choose to include and use it to respond and investigate. Please avoid sending sensitive calendar, location, or account information. During TestFlight testing, Apple may provide crash information, feedback, and screenshots or attachments you choose to share through TestFlight. Apple’s TestFlight terms describe that process. We use information received for support and app improvement, not advertising.

This website

These support and privacy pages do not use analytics scripts, advertising trackers, or a contact form. They are hosted through GitHub Pages. As with any hosted website, the hosting provider processes requests needed to serve the pages and may maintain operational logs. The site does not receive your saved widget content. Email links open your chosen email app.

Your controls

You can edit or delete saved designs in the app and change permissions in iOS or iPadOS Settings. Imported images and source caches may remain in local app storage after an individual design is deleted. Device backups and app-data removal are managed through the operating system. For questions about information you have emailed us directly, contact us below.

Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when the app’s practices change and revise its effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning information you have sent us directly, email littlewidgetlab@gmail.com.

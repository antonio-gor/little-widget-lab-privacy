A few good questions.

How do I change an existing widget?

Tap the design in My Widgets, edit it, and save. Touch and hold a library design to rename, duplicate, or delete it. You can restore deleted designs in Settings > Deleted Widgets until they are permanently deleted.

How do I connect Calendar, Weather, or Music?

Open the component in the editor. Choose your calendars, a city or Current Location, or a Music source. Optional access can be changed in iOS or iPadOS Settings. You can create widgets without connecting personal sources.

Why is Now Playing artwork behind the music?

Now Playing follows Apple Music, not every audio app. Open Little Widget Lab or tap Refresh Now Playing in its component editor. Player actions also request an update. iOS controls Home Screen refresh timing, so artwork may lag while the app is suspended.

Why does weather look out of date?

Check your connection and the selected location, then refresh Weather in the component editor. For Current Location, check Location Services access for Little Widget Lab, including widget access where offered. iOS schedules background refresh and may delay updates.

How does wallpaper matching work?

Choose Wallpaper as the widget background, then select a Home Screen screenshot and align its crop. Select a new screenshot if you change the wallpaper or widget position. It uses your selected image, not live transparency.

Why is a size or appearance missing?

Widget sizes and system appearances vary by device and OS. Little Widget Lab supports iOS 18 and iPadOS 18 or later. Newer appearances and larger sizes are available only on supported devices. Clear and Tinted are chosen in your Home Screen settings.

What should I include in a bug report?

Email your device, OS version, app version, and the steps that led to the issue. A screenshot or screen recording helps. Please remove private calendar events, precise locations, and account details. Beta testers can also send feedback through TestFlight.
